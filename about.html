Our Story

ချန်းလုံရွှေဆိုင်

Chan Lone Gold & Jewelery has been a trusted name for fine gold jewelry from our shop at C7, Ujana Plaza, 1st Floor, near Central Bank, Yangon. What began as a small family counter has grown into a destination for customers seeking quality gold pieces, honest craftsmanship, and personal service.

Every ring, necklace, bracelet and pair of earrings we offer reflects a blend of traditional Myanmar goldsmithing techniques and timeless design — pieces made to be worn daily, gifted for milestones, and passed down through generations.

We believe fine jewelry should feel personal. That's why we welcome every customer to visit us in person at Ujana Plaza, or reach out directly through Facebook Messenger to ask about a piece, request more photos, or discuss a custom order.
